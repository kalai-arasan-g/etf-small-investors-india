# Methodology and assumptions

1. **Period.** Daily data January 2015 – September 2026. Weights use a rolling 36-month window, so the out-of-sample period is January 2018 – September 2026 (105 months). Trades at month-end prices.
2. **Investors.** Lump sum (Rs 10,000 / 50,000 / 1,00,000 at the start) or monthly SIP (Rs 1,000 / 5,000 / 10,000). Main case: SIP Rs 5,000, zero brokerage, 20% slab.
3. **Whole units.** Units are bought in whole numbers; leftover cash earns nothing (cash drag is reported). Holdings are sold only if the excess over target exceeds 0.5% of the portfolio or the target is zero.
4. **ETF strategies.** Nifty 50 ETF only; equity ETFs 1/N (Nifty 50 + Next 50); multi-asset 1/N (five ETFs); minimum variance; maximum Sharpe; risk parity. Long-only, 40% cap for optimised weights, Ledoit–Wolf covariance (sample and DCC-GARCH as robustness). Annual rebalancing (monthly, quarterly and 5% threshold as robustness).
5. **Random stock portfolios.** 500 draws each of 5, 10, 15 and 20 stocks from the 500 most traded NSE stocks in January 2018 (3-month average traded value, price at least Rs 10); also from the 100 most traded. Equal weights, annual rebalancing, same contributions, costs and taxes. Delisted stocks are sold at the last price. The same draws are reused across scenarios.
6. **Risk decomposition.** Equal-weighted, frictionless, fractional basket of all stocks in each universe as a reference; compares Nifty 50 ETF → universe basket (systematic part) → random N-stock portfolio (firm-specific part); betas against the Nifty 50 ETF.
7. **Returns and measures.** Time-weighted monthly returns net of costs and taxes; Sharpe, Sortino, maximum drawdown, CVaR (5%), certainty-equivalent return, turnover, costs and tax as % of contributions and of gains, post-tax XIRR.
8. **Inference.** Ledoit–Wolf (2008) studentised circular block bootstrap for Sharpe-ratio differences and Ledoit–Wolf (2011) for variances (block length 5; 4,999 replications, 999 for each stock draw); Romano–Wolf (2005) stepdown adjusted p-values.
9. **Data cleaning.** (a) AMFI NAVs put on the split-adjusted per-unit basis using a 21-day median NAV/price ratio snapped to split factors; (b) ETF daily prices and NAVs: values more than 40% from the 11-day centred median replaced by interpolation (6 values in the published run); (c) stock monthly prices: one-month moves above 50% that reverse to within 20% the next month replaced by the geometric mean of neighbours (116 stock-months); (d) days on which ETF and index returns differ by more than 20% excluded from tracking error; (e) premium/discount values beyond ±50% treated as data errors. The G-Sec ETF is valued at NAV because its market price is stale (zero volume on 27.8% of days); market-price results are reported as robustness.
10. **Known limitations.** Stocks ranked by traded value (no free shares-outstanding data); ETFs selected with information not fully available in 2018 (all still exist today); one ETF per asset class; liquid-fund risk-free rate; simplified tax (no surcharge, single slab, terminal liquidation); random selection does not represent actual retail stock choices.
